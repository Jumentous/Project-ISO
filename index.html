Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) as its ad mediation platform, with Google AdMob connected as a partner-bidding network. Four rewarded video ad placements are available in the app — Offline 2x, Salvage 2x, Refit Skip, and Residuum Bonus — all of which are entirely optional and only triggered when you tap a button to watch one. The game does not show interstitial or banner ads of any kind.

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad mediation SDK and the ad networks it works with (including Google) may collect device information and advertising identifiers in order to select and deliver ads, consistent with their own data practices. For details on Google's role as an ad partner, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Account Data & Save Sync

The app offers optional cloud save syncing through Google Play Games Services sign-in, using Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication) to link your save to your Google Play Games account. Signing in is optional: you can play entirely offline with a local-only save, and nothing about the game is gated behind signing in.

If you choose to sign in, the app receives a Google Play Games account identifier (via a server auth code, exchanged through Unity Authentication) so it can associate your save data with your account. Your save data (game progress: resources, facilities, tech tree state, and similar in-game values) is then stored with Unity Gaming Services' Cloud Save so it can sync across devices signed into the same Google Play Games account. The app does not receive your Google account email address, contacts, or any other Google account data beyond what Play Games Services provides for sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy for Play Games Services, and Unity's Privacy Policy for Unity Gaming Services.

Analytics

The app does not currently collect any gameplay or usage analytics. No analytics SDK is active in the app at this time. If an analytics service is added in the future, this policy will be updated first to disclose what is collected.

In-App Purchases (Not Yet Live)

The app plans to offer optional in-app purchases through its in-game Store. Real-money billing is not yet active. Data Retention & Account Deletion

The app always stores your game progress locally on your device. If you never sign in with Google Play Games, that's the only copy that exists: there is no server-side save data, and uninstalling the app removes your local save data along with it.

If you choose to sign in, a synced copy of your save data is also retained in Unity Gaming Services' Cloud Save, tied to your Google Play Games account, for as long as you keep that data synced. You can stop syncing at any time by signing out in Settings, and you can request deletion of your synced save data using the details in the Contact section below.
