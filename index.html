Project ISO: Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy describes both features that are currently live and features that are planned but not yet released. Sections below are marked clearly where a feature has not launched yet. This document will be revised as those features (Cloud Save, GameAnalytics, in-app purchases) actually ship.

Advertising

Project ISO uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) as its ad mediation platform, with Google AdMob connected as a partner-bidding network. Four rewarded video ad placements are available in the app — Offline 2x, Salvage 2x, Refit Skip, and Residuum Bonus — all of which are entirely optional and only triggered when you tap a button to watch one. The game does not show interstitial or banner ads of any kind.

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad mediation SDK and the ad networks it works with (including Google) may collect device information and advertising identifiers in order to select and deliver ads, consistent with their own data practices. For details on Google's role as an ad partner, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Account Data & Save Sync (Not Yet Live)

Project ISO plans to offer cloud save syncing via Google Play Games Services sign-in, using Unity Authentication, so your progress can carry across devices. This feature has not launched yet. When it does, signing in will be optional, and this policy will be updated to describe exactly what account data is collected and how it's used before the feature goes live.

Analytics

Project ISO does not currently collect any gameplay or usage analytics. No analytics SDK is active in the app at this time. If an analytics service is added in the future, this policy will be updated first to disclose what is collected.

In-App Purchases (Not Yet Live)

Project ISO plans to offer optional in-app purchases through its in-game Store. Real-money billing is not yet active. If/when in-app purchases go live, all payment processing will be handled entirely by Google Play's own billing system — Project ISO itself does not receive, process, or store your payment card details.

Third-Party Services

Project ISO integrates with the following third-party services. This list covers only what is actually integrated today: anything planned but not yet live is marked accordingly, matching the rest of this policy.

  • Google / AdMob: used for rewarded video ad delivery via Unity LevelPlay, as described under Advertising above. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play (Not Yet Live): planned for Play Billing, to process in-app purchases once they launch (see In-App Purchases above). Payment processing will be handled entirely by Google Play; Project ISO itself does not receive, process, or store payment details.

Log Data

Using the app, particularly if an error occurs, may involve third-party code (currently, the ad mediation SDK described under Advertising above) automatically collecting information such as device model, operating system version, app configuration or state, and timestamps, for diagnostic purposes. This happens today because the ad SDK is integrated, not because of any dedicated analytics system: see Analytics above.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account creation is required to play.
  • No email address is collected by the game itself.
  • No user-generated content (chat, images, text, etc.) is collected or shared.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

Children's Privacy

Project ISO does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below so it can be addressed.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Currently, Project ISO stores your game progress locally on your device only. There is no account system today, so there is no server-side save data to retain or delete: uninstalling the app removes your local save data along with it.

Once Cloud Save (Not Yet Live) launches (see Account Data & Save Sync above), save data tied to your Play Games sign-in will be retained for as long as your account remains active. At that point, you will be able to request deletion of that data using the details in the Contact section below.

Security

We use reasonable, commercially acceptable measures to help protect the information described in this policy. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

As noted above, this policy will be revised as features actually ship. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the date of the most recent revision.
