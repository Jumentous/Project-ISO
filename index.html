Umbral Idle: Last Salvage

Sci-fi idle colony builder for Android

Umbral Idle: Last Salvage was developed under the working title "Project ISO," which is why that name may still appear in older store listings or files. Where a feature is integrated but not yet switched on, the section below says so.

Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) as its ad mediation platform, with Google AdMob connected as a partner-bidding network. Six rewarded video ad placements exist in the app (Offline 2x, Salvage 2x, Refit Skip, Residuum Bonus, Fleet Charge, and Matter Lump), all entirely optional and only triggered when you tap a button to watch one. The game does not show interstitial or banner ads of any kind.

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad mediation SDK and the ad networks it works with (including Google) may collect device information and advertising identifiers in order to select and deliver ads, consistent with their own data practices. For details on Google's role as an ad partner, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Account Data & Save Sync

The app offers optional cloud save syncing through Google Play Games Services sign-in, using Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication) to link your save to your Google Play Games account. Signing in is optional: you can play entirely offline with a local-only save, and nothing about the game is gated behind signing in.

If you choose to sign in, the app receives a Google Play Games account identifier (via a server auth code, exchanged through Unity Authentication) so it can associate your save data with your account. Your save data (game progress: resources, facilities, tech tree state, and similar in-game values) is then stored with Unity Gaming Services' Cloud Save so it can sync across devices signed into the same Google Play Games account. While signed in, the app also reports achievement unlocks and four leaderboard scores (lifetime Salvage, Iso-Cores, Collapses, and lifetime Insights, all derived from your game progress) to Google Play Games so they appear in your Play Games profile; your display name and avatar on those leaderboards come from Google, not from the app. The app does not receive your Google account email address, contacts, or any other Google account data beyond what Play Games Services provides for sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy for Play Games Services, and Unity's Privacy Policy for Unity Gaming Services.

Analytics

The app integrates the GameAnalytics SDK to understand how the game is played and balanced. It is switched on only after the consent prompt described below has been answered, and never if you decline. When active, it records anonymous gameplay events (for example: tutorial steps completed, tiers unlocked, Collapses performed, rewarded ads watched, daily rewards claimed, and time away from the game) together with device information such as device model, operating system version, app version, a randomly generated install identifier, and session timings. Events contain in-game numbers only: no name, email address, contacts, precise location, or content you type is ever sent. See GameAnalytics' Privacy Policy.

Crash Reporting

The app uses Unity's built-in engine diagnostics to report crashes and unhandled errors so they can be fixed. A report contains technical information about the failure (stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, memory state) and no personal information beyond what is needed to identify the device class. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The app can schedule optional local reminders on your device (for example when a Salvage Run has returned or the Collapse floor is reached). These are created and stored entirely on your device; no push notification service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off individually or entirely in Settings, or through the system notification settings.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases through its in-game Store, processed entirely by Google Play's own billing system: the app itself never receives, processes, or stores your payment card details. Purchases you own are restored automatically from Google Play when you reinstall or change device.

Third-Party Services (Umbral Idle: Last Salvage)

  • Google / AdMob: rewarded video ad delivery via Unity LevelPlay, as described under Advertising above.
  • Google Play Games Services: the optional sign-in described under Account Data & Save Sync above.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Cloud Save): links your optional sign-in to your synced save data.
  • Google Play Billing: processes in-app purchases.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: the regional consent prompt described under Consent below.
  • GameAnalytics: consent-gated gameplay analytics, as described under Analytics above.
  • Unity engine diagnostics: crash and error reporting.

Shared Privacy Terms

These sections apply to every game covered by this policy.

Consent

On first launch, each game shows its own plain consent choice (Accept or Decline). Your choice controls whether analytics is switched on at all: if you decline, the analytics SDK is never initialised on your device. Where a game also uses advertising (currently Umbral Idle: Last Salvage only), your choice also controls whether the advertising SDKs may use your data for personalised ads. This dialog is shown to every player in every region: it normally defers to a regional consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which only prompts players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland and applies a non-personalised default everywhere else, but that form is not currently available for either game, so each game's own dialog is shown to everyone instead until it is. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, by clearing the app's data, or by contacting us (see Contact below).

Log Data

Using a game, particularly if an error occurs, may involve the third-party SDKs listed above automatically collecting information such as device model, operating system version, app configuration or state, IP address, and timestamps, for diagnostic purposes and to operate their services. Separately, on launch and when returning from the background, each game makes a lightweight request to a Google server purely to read the current time from the response header; this is used to keep offline earnings and daily rewards honest if the device clock has been changed. That request carries no account or game data.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account creation is required to play any of our games.
  • No email address is collected by any game itself.
  • No user-generated content (chat, images, text, etc.) is collected or shared.
  • No precise or approximate location is collected by any game. (Ad and analytics partners may infer a coarse region from your IP address under their own policies.)
  • No data is sold, and none is shared with third parties for their own advertising purposes beyond the ad delivery described above.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in any of our games. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below so it can be addressed.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Every game covered by this policy always stores your game progress locally on your device first. If you never sign in with Google Play Games, that's the only copy that exists: there is no server-side save data, and uninstalling the app removes your local save data along with it. If you choose to sign in, a synced copy of your save data is also retained in Unity Gaming Services' Cloud Save, tied to your Google Play Games account, for as long as you keep that data synced. You can stop syncing at any time by signing out in Settings, and you can request deletion of your synced save data using the details in the Contact section below.

Security

We use reasonable, commercially acceptable measures to help protect the information described in this policy. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
